01 / About

About

I’m a sophomore at Villanova studying mechanical engineering, and I want to build race cars for a living.

I joined NovaRacing, Villanova’s Formula SAE team, in October 2025. I worked on drivetrain design, manufacturing and assembly for last season’s car, VU17, and helped build more than 30 carbon-fiber aerodynamic components across subsystems. This year I’m the lead design engineer for the drivetrain subsystem on VU18, and I’m aiming for a hands-on role in aero design and manufacturing as well.

Outside the race shop, I’m an undergraduate researcher in Villanova’s ME³ Lab, building an electrochemical test setup to measure hydrogen permeation in structural metals. I’m looking for a Summer 2027 placement in motorsport, ideally in Formula 1 or another top-level racing series.
