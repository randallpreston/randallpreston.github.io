NovaRacing FSAE Spring 2026–Present In design

VU18 Drivetrain Design

VU18 is NovaRacing’s current design cycle. As lead design engineer for the drivetrain subsystem, I’m individually overseeing the iterative design of the car’s drivetrain, with a focus on taking mass and cost out of the system.

Role
Lead Design Engineer, Drivetrain
Timeline
Spring 2026–Present
Team
NovaRacing FSAE · Powertrain
Status
In design
Project Updates

Headline result

58%

Projected reduction in half-shaft mass from replacing the steel half-shafts with a composite design.

Projection. Planned upgrade, not yet manufactured.

Focus areas

What I’m working on

Three workstreams, all aimed at a lighter, cheaper and better-optimized drivetrain.

01

Iterative system design

Individually overseeing the iterative design process for the full drivetrain system as the subsystem’s lead.

02

Composite half-shafts

Planning the switch from steel to composite half-shafts, for a projected 58% reduction in half-shaft mass.

03

Additively manufactured mounts

Organizing the additive manufacturing process for the differential–engine mounts. Printing them opens the door to topology optimization and further mass and cost reduction.

Design in progress

More coming as VU18 develops

CAD, analysis and test results will be added here as VU18 moves from design into manufacturing. Alongside drivetrain, I’m aiming for an active role in aero design and manufacturing on this car.
