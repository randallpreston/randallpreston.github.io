VU17 Design & Manufacturing

VU17 is NovaRacing’s 2026 Formula SAE car, which competed at FSAE Michigan. I joined the team in October 2025 as a drivetrain design engineer and worked on the design, manufacturing and assembly of drivetrain components. I also joined an inter-system effort that manufactured more than 30 carbon-fiber aerodynamic components for VU17 and a Senior Capstone Project.

01 / Aerodynamics Manufacturing

Aerodynamics Manufacturing

Aero is its own subsystem on NovaRacing, but building a full aero package takes help from across the team. Working alongside the aero group, I helped manufacture carbon-fiber components for VU17, from tooling and layup through vacuum bagging, demolding and final assembly.

More than 30 components came out of that effort, for VU17 and for a Senior Capstone Project. This season I’m aiming for a larger role in aero design and manufacturing alongside my drivetrain work.

Processes

  • Wet layup
  • Resin infusion
  • Vacuum bagging
  • Post-processing
  • Assembly

30+

Carbon-fiber components manufactured

2

Programs supported: VU17 and a Senior Capstone

  1. Step 01

    Tooling

    A composite part is only as good as its mold. Aero molds start on a CNC router, followed by hands-on mold work in the shop. The undertray mold was built up from stacked MDF.

  2. Step 02

    Layup & vacuum bagging

    Carbon fiber is laid into the mold, then sealed under a vacuum bag. Atmospheric pressure consolidates the laminate against the mold while the resin cures.

  3. Step 03

    Cure & demold

    Once cured, parts come off the mold and move on to trimming and finishing.

  4. Step 04

    Post-processing & assembly

    Finished elements are trimmed, fitted with mounting hardware, assembled into complete wings and installed on the car.

02 / Drivetrain Design

Drivetrain Design

The drivetrain carries power from the engine to the rear wheels. On VU17 I took part in goal-oriented design, manufacturing and assembly of its components. That season set up my role as the drivetrain lead for VU18.

Scope

  • Goal-oriented component design
  • Manufacturing and assembly
  • Physical testing of hardware

03 / At competition

FSAE Michigan

VU17 competed at Formula SAE Michigan, held at Michigan International Speedway in May 2026.

During the competition, the car had an engine failure. That night I stayed up until 5 a.m. helping the team swap out the engine in the paddock. It was one of the best learning experiences of my season.

Competition week

  • Design presentation prep with the team ahead of the judging round
